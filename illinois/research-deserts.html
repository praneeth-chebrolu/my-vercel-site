How a “desert” is measured

Burden

Real per-county cancer incidence — age-adjusted rate and average annual case count — from NCI & CDC State Cancer Profiles (All Cancer Sites, 2018–2022).

Access

For each county we find the nearest of 12 real Illinois trial centers, estimate the drive, and count active trials reachable within 30 / 60 / 90 minutes.

Severity score

Severity = √(burden × access gap), where the access gap blends drive time (55%) and scarcity of nearby trials (45%). Higher = greater unmet need.
